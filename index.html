Frequently Asked Questions

What you need to know

What types of businesses do you work with?

I specialize in bookkeeping for independent professionals, established business owners, professional service providers and growing companies that value accurate financial management and a dedicated professional relationship.

How much does bookkeeping cost?

Monthly bookkeeping packages start at $295 for Essential, $550 for Professional and $795 for Business Plus. Final monthly fees depend on transaction volume, account complexity and the services your business requires. All prices are in USD.

What happens if I exceed my monthly transaction allowance?

Additional monthly transactions are priced from $2 per transaction. If your business consistently exceeds its agreed allowance, your package can be reviewed. Businesses with more than 600 monthly transactions receive a custom quote.

Are there additional onboarding or setup fees?

New client onboarding starts at $250. QuickBooks setup and configuration, where required, starts at $350. Applicable fees will be discussed before your engagement begins.

Can you help if my bookkeeping is behind?

Yes. Historical bookkeeping and catch-up services start at $200 per month of backlog. Bookkeeping cleanup and corrections start at $100 per hour. The required work and applicable fees are agreed before work begins.

Do I need to use QuickBooks?

I discuss your existing accounting software and bookkeeping arrangements during the introductory consultation to establish a suitable approach.

Are payroll and tax preparation included?

Monthly bookkeeping packages focus on maintaining accurate financial records and providing the agreed bookkeeping and reporting services. Payroll processing, 1099 preparation and filing, tax preparation, and specialized services are quoted separately.

What communication and support are included?

Essential includes email support. Professional and Business Plus include priority email and text support during business hours only, together with financial reviews according to the selected package.

Who will manage my bookkeeping?

You work directly with me, Jeanette. My boutique approach prioritizes accountability, clear communication and personalized attention throughout your engagement.

How do I get started?

Book a free 30-minute consultation to discuss your business and bookkeeping requirements. Together, we can establish whether my services are a suitable fit for your business.
