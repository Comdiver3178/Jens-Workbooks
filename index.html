Frequently Asked Questions

What you need to know

What types of businesses do you work with?

Our services are designed for independent professionals, photographers, creative businesses, professional service providers and small to growing companies.

How much does bookkeeping cost?

Monthly fees depend on the volume of transactions, complexity of your accounts and the services you require. Pricing is confirmed before the engagement begins.

Can you help if my bookkeeping is behind?

Cleanup and catch-up services are available. The required work is assessed separately and a scope and fee are agreed before work commences.

Do I need to use QuickBooks?

Your existing accounting software and bookkeeping arrangements will be discussed during the introductory consultation to establish a suitable approach.

How do I get started?

Contact us to discuss your business and bookkeeping requirements. We can then establish whether our services are a suitable fit for your needs.
